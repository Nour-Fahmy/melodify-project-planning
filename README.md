# Melodify Music Player — Project Planning Case Study

An academic software engineering planning exercise for a proposed music player with lyric sharing, timed listening sessions, account management, and an AI-assisted playlist feature. The work demonstrates project scope definition, task decomposition, schedule and cost planning, risk and quality planning, and requirements traceability.

**Prepared by:** Nour Waleed, Hagar Aly, and Ahmed Khalid · May 2026

> **Status:** Planning artifacts for a course scenario. This repository does not contain application source code or evidence that Melodify was built, launched, funded, or sponsored. Names of sponsors or outside stakeholders in the submitted documents are scenario assumptions, not claims of affiliation.

## Scenario scope

The plans cover account registration and login, user profiles, music playback controls, lyric annotation and sharing, AI-generated listening sessions, an administrative dashboard, testing, and launch preparation. The cost and success figures in the source documents are **planning assumptions and targets**, not measured results.

## Reading the submitted artifacts

The documents preserve their original course-work content. **Their schedule baselines are inconsistent:** some plans state a May 1, 2026 deadline, while the supplied Gantt chart places launch work in June 2026. The separately supplied critical path analysis describes 111 working days and does not reconcile with the Gantt chart. Treat each as an example of a planning method; do not combine their dates or durations into one validated baseline.

The original project charter and network diagram are not included in this public selection. The charter asserts a professional credential and sponsor relationship that are not verified here; the network image does not render its connections legibly. This repository focuses on the readable planning artifacts.
